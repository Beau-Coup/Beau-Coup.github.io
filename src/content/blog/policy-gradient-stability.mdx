I gave a chalk talk to the lab last week. These are my notes, mostly cleaned up. The talk was supposed to be about our L4DC paper but it turned into a list of things we still cannot prove.

There are roughly three regimes where the analysis goes through cleanly: linear dynamics, contractive dynamics, and dynamics where we already have a Lyapunov function and the policy is a small perturbation of a known stabilizing controller. Outside of those, we are mostly waving our hands.
