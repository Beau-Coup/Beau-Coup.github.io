excerpt: "A short note about a paper that did not get in, and what I learned from reading the reviews three months later."
---

The paper got rejected. The first time I read the reviews I was furious for two days. The second time, three months later, I noticed the reviewers were mostly right.

The hardest one to accept was the methodological reviewer who said the experiments did not actually test the claim. They didn't. We had written the claim too broadly because the broader version sounded better.
