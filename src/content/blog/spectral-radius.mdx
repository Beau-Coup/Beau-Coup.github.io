A small list of facts I have looked up more than five times this year. I am writing them down in the hope that the act of typing them will lodge them somewhere more permanent than my browser history.

The spectral radius equals the limit of the n-th root of the operator norm of A^n. That is Gelfand's formula. The spectral radius itself is not, in general, an operator norm. I keep forgetting this.
