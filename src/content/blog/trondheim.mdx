Trondheim in late December has about four hours of usable daylight and I love it. My mother makes the same lefse recipe she has made since I was small and refuses to let me help with the dough.

I brought a notebook and a copy of Folland and got through two chapters in the dark afternoons. It was the most productive week of my year.
