I've started and abandoned this proof four times in the last year. The trick, for me, was to stop trying to memorize the construction of the outer measure and instead to ask: what is the smallest σ-algebra on which my pre-measure is honest?

Here is the embarrassing thing. For about eighteen months I had the diagram in my head pointing the wrong way. I thought the outer measure was the goal and Carathéodory's criterion was a technicality. It is the other way around. The criterion is the whole point. The outer measure is just a candidate.

Once I flipped the diagram, the proof became almost mechanical. I'm leaving this here partly so I can find it again next time I forget.

$$
x^2 = E m G
$$
